## SovietLean — Soviet Science, Verified

**[SovietLean](https://michalshavitnyu.github.io/soviet-science-lean/)** (*Soviet Science, Verified*) is an open dataset of Soviet mathematics and physics: English translations paired with Lean 4 formalizations of the main results. It covers turbulence, probability, analysis and high-energy physics. Each theorem's status (proved, or stated with the proof still open) is checked by machine on every change, and the whole collection can be downloaded as a [dataset](https://michalshavitnyu.github.io/soviet-science-lean/dataset.html).

Contributions are welcome: see the [contributor guide](https://michalshavitnyu.github.io/soviet-science-lean/contribute.html) or the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/michalshavitNYU/soviet-science-lean).

## Turbulence translations

English translations of works by Soviet and Russian mathematicians, with a focus on turbulence and the Kolmogorov school, are collected in the [soviet-turbulence](https://github.com/michalshavitNYU/soviet-turbulence) repository.
